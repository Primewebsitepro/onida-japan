Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order a product?

Browse the catalog and tap Call or WhatsApp on any product card. Our team confirms availability, pricing and delivery with you directly — no online checkout needed.

Yes, we coordinate delivery nationwide from our Costa del Este office. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll confirm the details.

Payment options are confirmed directly with our sales team when you place your order — just ask when you reach out on WhatsApp or by phone.

Products carry manufacturer warranty coverage. Our team will share the specific terms for each item when you enquire.

Of course — we're at Peninsula Center, Office 406, Costa del Este, República de Panamá. We recommend messaging ahead on WhatsApp.
